    How it works

    Teach the hotel what matters.

    1. Connect

      Each big load runs through a hub channel.

    2. Rank

      Guest rooms and the elevator first. The heater and laundry can wait.

    3. Protect

      Near the limit, MaTak pauses the lowest priority, then resumes it when there is room.

    4. Learn

      It spots staff routines and suggests new rules. One tap to approve.

    5. Save

      It switches off what staff forget and runs heavy loads at cheaper hours.

    Our promise: protection is included in every plan. Guest comfort is never behind a paywall.
